Preamble

We, the founders and developers of D-TECH, in order to establish a unified ecosystem, ensure innovation, promote ethical conduct, and secure the rights and responsibilities of all users, do ordain and establish this General Law of D-TECH. This document serves as the supreme rulebook and overarching constitution for the entire D-TECH Ecosystem—including all platforms, tools, websites, apps, services, and communities.

Article I: Scope and Definitions

  • "The D-TECH Ecosystem" refers collectively to all D-TECH entities, platforms, tools, websites, apps, services, communications, and digital infrastructure under the D-TECH banner.
  • "Citizen/User" refers to any individual, bot, or entity that accesses, interacts with, or utilizes any part of the D-TECH Ecosystem.
  • "The General Law" refers to this foundational rulebook, binding upon all users unconditionally.

Article II: Core Principles & Acceptable Conduct

Every Citizen of the D-TECH Ecosystem has the right to use our services safely and securely. In return, all users must adhere to the highest standards of conduct. Users shall not:

  • Engage in illegal, fraudulent, or unethical activities.
  • Attempt to modify, reverse-engineer, exploit, or disrupt any D-TECH infrastructure or software.
  • Distribute, resell, or share D-TECH products or access without explicit, written authorization from D-TECH Command.
  • Use the D-TECH Ecosystem to harm, harass, or maliciously target others.

Article III: Intellectual Property & Sovereignty

D-TECH retains absolute sovereignty over its creations. All tools, software, code, branding, logos, and innovations are the exclusive intellectual property of D-TECH. Unauthorized reproduction, modification, or uncredited use of our intellectual property is a direct violation of this Law and will be met with immediate enforcement.

Article IV: Enforcement & Revocation of Access

D-TECH reserves the unalienable right to police its Ecosystem. Violations of the General Law will result in immediate consequences, which may include but are not limited to:

  • Permanent revocation of access (banishment/blacklisting) from all D-TECH platforms.
  • Public disclosure of severe infractions to protect the community.
  • Pursuit of civil and criminal legal action against malicious actors.

Article V: Limitation of Liability

The D-TECH Ecosystem and all related services are provided "as is," without warranties of any kind. D-TECH and its founders shall bear no liability for any direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, losses, or legal repercussions arising from the use or misuse of our tools and services.

Article VI: Privacy & Data Protection

By entering the D-TECH Ecosystem, users acknowledge and accept our prevailing Privacy Mandates. D-TECH respects user data and outlines specific data collection, usage, and protection protocols within its respective project Privacy Policies, all of which are subordinate to this General Law.

Article VII: Amendments to the Law

As the D-TECH Ecosystem evolves, so too must its laws. D-TECH command reserves the right to amend, update, or append this Core Constitution at any time. Continued participation in the Ecosystem following any amendments constitutes absolute acceptance of the revised General Law.
